Privacy Policy for Slide Puzzle

This privacy policy describes how Slide Puzzle ("the app", "we") handles information when you use our mobile application.

1. Data we collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data ourselves.

  • No account registration is required.
  • No names, emails, phone numbers, or contacts are collected.
  • All game progress (completed levels, unlocked skins, stars, settings, and daily streaks) is stored locally on your device using the app's private storage. This data never leaves your device and is deleted if you uninstall the app.

2. Third-party services — Start.io

The app displays advertisements provided by Start.io (StartApp). To serve, measure, and improve ads, Start.io and its advertising partners may automatically collect and process:

  • Device identifiers (e.g., Android Advertising ID)
  • IP address (used to estimate general location, e.g., country)
  • Ad interaction data (ad views, taps, impressions)
  • Device and app information (model, OS version, app version, crash data)

This data is processed by Start.io under its own policies:

  • Start.io Privacy Policy
  • Start.io Publisher Terms

You can opt out of personalized advertising in your device settings:
Settings → Google → Ads → Delete advertising ID (or "Opt out of Ads Personalization"). You will still see ads, but they will not be based on your interests.

3. Children's privacy

The app is a casual puzzle game suitable for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Since the app does not collect personal data directly, no children's data is stored by us. Ad content is served by Google AdMob in accordance with its policies.

4. Permissions

The app requests only:

  • Internet access — required to load advertisements.
  • Network state — to check connectivity before loading ads.
  • Vibration — optional haptic feedback (can be disabled in Settings).

No permission is used to collect personal information.

5. Data retention and deletion

Since all game data is stored locally on your device, you can delete it at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app. We hold no server-side copies of your data.

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

7. Contact

1. Datos que recopilamos

No recopilamos, almacenamos ni transmitimos datos personales.

  • No se requiere registro.
  • No recopilamos nombres, correos, teléfonos ni contactos.
  • Todo el progreso del juego (niveles completados, skins, estrellas, ajustes y rachas diarias) se guarda localmente en tu dispositivo. Estos datos nunca salen de tu dispositivo y se eliminan si desinstalas la aplicación.

2. Servicios de terceros — Start.io

La aplicación muestra anuncios de Start.io (StartApp). Para servir y medir anuncios, Start.io y sus socios publicitarios pueden recopilar automáticamente:

  • Identificadores del dispositivo (ID de publicidad de Android)

Puedes desactivar la publicidad personalizada en:
Ajustes → Google → Anuncios → Eliminar ID de publicidad.

3. Privacidad infantil

No recopilamos deliberadamente información personal de menores. La app no almacena datos de ningún usuario en servidores.

4. Permisos

5. Conservación y eliminación de datos

Todos los datos del juego son locales. Puedes eliminarlos borrando los datos de la app o desinstalándola.
